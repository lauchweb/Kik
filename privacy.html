Privacy

We respect your privacy and aim to keep things simple. This Privacy page explains how Kiksoz may handle information when you visit and use our website or choose to contact us.

Information You Provide

Kiksoz does not operate an online shop or require you to create an account. We do not ask you to provide personal information simply to browse the website.

If you choose to contact us using the contact details provided on the website, we may receive the information you decide to share with us, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, basic technical information may be processed when you visit Kiksoz. This can include information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and general technical information needed to operate, secure, and improve the website.

How We Use Information

Information we receive may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate with you when necessary, maintain and secure the website, and understand how the website is used so that we can improve it.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the reason it was provided unless we have a legitimate reason to do so or are required to by law.

Third-Party Services and Links

The Kiksoz website may contain links to third-party websites or services, including Instagram. When you follow one of these links, you are leaving the Kiksoz website and the privacy practices of that third party apply.

Keeping Information Safe

We take reasonable steps to protect information that comes into our possession. However, no website or method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

How Long We Keep Information

Information provided to us is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, to maintain appropriate records, or to meet legal obligations where applicable.

Questions About Privacy

If you have any questions about how we handle information, please email us about our Privacy Policy.
