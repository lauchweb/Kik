About Kiksoz

Kiksoz is about discovering what’s worth finding.

We are product-oriented by nature. We like products, we notice them, we talk about them, and we are always curious about what is out there. We look for things that stand out, whether they are useful, unusual, beautifully made, or simply a great find.

The idea behind Kiksoz started around the world, shaped by the places, products, and discoveries that inspired us along the way. But the adventure started here in Mauritius.

From here, we explore, search, and discover — always looking for something worth bringing to retail.

We also believe that the way people are treated matters. From the first moment to the final step, we want things to feel clear, easy, and enjoyable, with attention to the little details that make people want to come back.

We are Kiksoz. And we are just getting started.
